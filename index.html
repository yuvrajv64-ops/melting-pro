FeO Lab Test (Slag & Sponge)

Same KMnO₄ titration method for slag & sponge iron.

Procedure:

1. Grind sample finely.

2. Dry if moisture present.

3. Take 0.5g sample.

4. Add dilute H₂SO₄.

5. Heat mildly.

6. Titrate with 0.1N KMnO₄.

7. Light pink endpoint.

Formula: FeO% = (V × 1.6)
