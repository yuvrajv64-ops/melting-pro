FeO Wet Lab Method (KMnO₄ Titration)

Principle: Fe²⁺ in slag reacts with KMnO₄ in acidic medium.

Procedure:

1. Take fresh slag sample and grind properly.

2. Weigh 0.5 g sample accurately (W).

3. Add dilute H₂SO₄.

4. Heat mildly to dissolve Fe²⁺.

5. Titrate with 0.1N KMnO₄.

6. Note titre value (V ml).

Accurate Formula:

FeO% = (V × N × 7.99 × 100) / W

Shortcut (0.5g sample + 0.1N):

FeO% ≈ V × 1.6

Sponge Iron FeO Check

Indirect Method:

FeO % = Total Fe % − Metallic Fe %

Example:

Total Fe = 90%

Fe(M) = 82%

FeO = 8%

FeO Interpretation Guide

Below 10% → Reducing / Safe

10–15% → Moderate Oxidation

Above 15% → High Oxidation Risk
