🧪 FeO & Slag Science

FeO Lab Test (KMnO₄ Method)

Principle: Fe²⁺ in slag is titrated with KMnO₄ in acidic medium.

Procedure:

1. Take 0.5g finely ground slag sample.

2. Add dilute H₂SO₄.

3. Heat mildly to dissolve.

4. Titrate with 0.1N KMnO₄.

5. Note light pink endpoint.

Accurate Formula:

FeO% = (V × N × 7.99 × 100) / W

Shortcut (0.5g & 0.1N): FeO% ≈ V × 1.6
