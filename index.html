🧪 FeO Wet Analysis

Wet Chemical KMnO₄ Method

1. Take 0.5g finely ground slag/sponge sample.

2. Add dilute H₂SO₄.

3. Heat mildly to dissolve Fe²⁺.

4. Titrate with 0.1N KMnO₄.

5. Light pink endpoint.

Accurate Formula:

FeO% = (V × N × 7.99 × 100) / W

Slag Identification

Reducing Slag: Dark/Black → Best for Mn

Normal Slag: Brown → Skim before Mn

Oxidizing Slag: Red/Orange → Avoid Mn
