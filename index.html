🧪 FeO Lab Test Method (KMnO₄ Titration)

Principle: Fe²⁺ in slag is titrated with KMnO₄ in acidic medium.

Procedure:

1. Take fresh slag sample and grind properly.

2. Weigh 0.5 g sample (W).

3. Dissolve in dilute H₂SO₄.

4. Titrate with 0.1N KMnO₄.

5. Note titre value (V ml).

Accurate Formula:

FeO% = (V × N × 7.99 × 100) / W

Shortcut (For 0.5g sample & 0.1N KMnO₄):

FeO% ≈ V × 1.6
