🧪 FeO (KMnO₄ Method – BIS Full)

Principle: Fe²⁺ is oxidized by KMnO₄ in acidic medium.

Reagents:

• 0.1N KMnO₄ → 3.16g/L (standardize before use)

• Dilute H₂SO₄ (1:1)

• Distilled water

Procedure:

1. 0.5g fresh finely ground sample weigh karo

2. Conical flask me lo

3. 20–30 ml H₂SO₄ add karo

4. Mild heat (Fe²⁺ dissolve)

5. Immediately KMnO₄ titration karo

6. Light pink endpoint (30 sec stable)

Reaction:

5Fe²⁺ + MnO₄⁻ + 8H⁺ → 5Fe³⁺ + Mn²⁺

Formula:

FeO% = (V × N × 7.99 × 100) / W

Shortcut: FeO ≈ V × 1.6

Precautions:

• Fresh sample use karo

• Air exposure avoid karo

• Delay mat karo

🧪 Total Iron (FeT – Dichromate BIS Full)

Principle: All Fe converted to Fe²⁺ then titrated with K₂Cr₂O₇

Reagents:

• 0.1N K₂Cr₂O₇ → 4.903g/L

• SnCl₂ solution (10%)

• HgCl₂ solution

• Diphenylamine indicator

• HCl (1:1)

Procedure:

1. 0.5g sample weigh karo

2. HCl me dissolve karo

3. Heat till complete dissolution

4. SnCl₂ dropwise add karo (yellow color disappear)

5. HgCl₂ add karo (excess SnCl₂ remove)

6. Cool karo

7. Indicator add karo

8. Dichromate se titration

Endpoint: Green → Violet

Formula:

Fe% = (V × N × 5.585 × 100) / W

Shortcut: FeT ≈ V × 1.117

Precautions:

• SnCl₂ excess avoid karo

• HgCl₂ immediately add karo

🧪 Sponge Iron FeO (Indirect Method)

Principle:

FeO calculated from Total Fe and Metallic Fe

Formula:

FeO% = (FeT – FeM) × 1.286

Example:

FeT = 90%, FeM = 82%

FeO = 10.29%

Note:

Direct KMnO₄ method not accurate for sponge iron

🧪 SiO₂ (Gravimetric BIS Full)

Reagents:

• HCl, HNO₃

Procedure:

1. Sample dissolve karo (acid)

2. Evaporate to dryness

3. Bake (100–120°C)

4. Add HCl + water

5. Filter silica residue

6. Ignite at 1000°C

7. Weigh residue

Formula:

SiO₂% = residue ×100 / sample

Note: High accuracy method

🧪 CaO (EDTA BIS Full)

Reagents:

• 0.01M EDTA

• NH₄OH buffer (pH 12)

• Murexide indicator

Procedure:

1. Sample dissolve karo

2. pH ~12 adjust karo

3. Indicator add karo

4. EDTA titration

Endpoint: Pink → Purple

Formula:

CaO% = (V × N × 28 × 100) / W

Shortcut: CaO ≈ V × 2.8

🧪 MgO (EDTA BIS Full)

Reagents:

• EDTA

• EBT indicator

Procedure:

1. Same solution use karo

2. Indicator add karo

3. EDTA titration

Endpoint: Wine red → Blue

Formula:

MgO% = (V × N × 20.15 × 100) / W

Shortcut: MgO ≈ V × 2.0

📌 Practical Testing Guide

✔ Slag → FeO direct KMnO₄ method

✔ Sponge Iron → FeT & FeM method

✔ Mill Scale → Approx KMnO₄ (error possible)

Tip: Fresh sample = best accuracy
