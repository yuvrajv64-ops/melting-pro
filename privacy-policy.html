Privacy Policy for MeltingPro

MeltingPro (developed by Aarav Steel Tech) is an offline industrial calculator app designed for induction furnace operations. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from users.

Information Collection

The app does not collect personal information, device information, or usage analytics. All calculations run offline on the user’s device.

Data Usage

All inputs such as weight, FeO values, Mn%, and carbon values remain on the device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use ads, tracking SDKs, or third-party APIs.

Offline Mode

MeltingPro works fully offline after installation.
